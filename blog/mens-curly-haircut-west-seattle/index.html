Curly haircuts for men.

Curly hair on men often gets cut like straight hair, and then it grows out into a shape nobody asked for. At Pinup Salon in West Seattle, a few of our stylists cut curls for a living, and men are welcome in every one of those chairs.

Ask for the cut that matches your hair.

Our Dapper Gentleman Cut is the men’s haircut on the menu. If your curls are the main event, you can book the Curl Expert Haircut instead. That cut is done dry, then your hair is cleansed, conditioned and set.

Denika is a barber who loves curly hair.

Denika has been in the industry since 2010 and joined Pinup in 2024. She is a skilled barber and she enjoys curly textures, so she knows both the clipper side and the curl side.

Jen and Bre cut men’s hair too.

Jen, the owner, counts curl cutting and men’s cuts among her favorite services. Bre lists men’s grooming among her specialties, along with precision cuts.

Want more curl than you have?

Our Guys and Girls Love Curls service is a perm, beach wave or texture wave. It is for anyone who wants more wave than their hair has on its own.

If you are not sure which cut to book, start with a consultation. Tell your stylist how you wear your hair on a normal day, and they will tell you what will actually work. You can book online or call (206) 932-5077.
