The Pinup experience

Like your best friend’s house

Pinup is an intimate salon for people who want real one-on-one time with their stylist, without the hubbub of a high-traffic salon.

Jen named the salon for the pinup girls of the 1940s. Clients come to us for every era, from 1920s finger waves to 1960s volume, and for modern color and cuts too.

Curl Expert services

Curls, cut the way they grow

The Curl Expert Haircut is cut dry, then cleansed, conditioned and set. Cutting dry gives each curl custom movement, blending and texture.

Jen, Denika and Bre all cut curls, and we can hand paint highlights made just for curly hair.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Pinup Salon?

Pinup Salon is at 4151 Fauntleroy Way SW, Seattle, WA 98126, in West Seattle. Call (206) 932-5077 or book online.

What are Pinup Salon’s hours?

Pinup is open seven days a week: Monday 10am to 6pm, Tuesday 10am to 8pm, Wednesday 10am to 8pm, Thursday 9am to 7pm, Friday 9am to 8pm, Saturday 8am to 7:30pm, Sunday 9am to 6pm.

Do you cut curly hair?

Yes. The Curl Expert Haircut (starting at $115) is a dry haircut followed by a cleanse, condition and curl set. Jen, Denika and Bre all cut curls.

Do you do hair extensions?

Yes. Lindsey is certified in Great Lengths and Hair Dreams keratin bonded extensions, and the salon also does tape-ins, I-tips and sew-ins. Most extensions are priced at a consultation.

Can you do a vintage hairstyle?

Yes. The Vintage Look (starting at $100) is a classic finger wave or pin-curl set and style. Updos and roller sets are starting at $85.

Do you do wedding hair?

Yes. Wed Head is starting at $250 and includes a one hour trial run before the day. On-site bridal hair or makeup is starting at $325.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Cuts for kids under 10 are starting at $27, and tween and young teen cuts are starting at $38. Denika takes extra care with children and neurodivergent clients.

Do you do men’s haircuts?

Yes. The Dapper Gentleman Cut is starting at $45. Denika is a skilled barber and Bre specializes in men’s grooming.
